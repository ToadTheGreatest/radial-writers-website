RADIAL WRITERS
About Us
Radial Writers is a small musical group where we like to make aviation parodies of songs that we like. We find the intrumental track online, we write some lyrics, and when we meet up at EAA Airventure Oshkosh, we sing it together in one of the member's airplanes. Meanwhile, at Airventure, we go around and take pictures of aircraft, so we can edit the photos together so it is synced with the music track.
